# Fantasy Football Matrix™ Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fantasy Football Matrix™ (the “App”), owned by Brett Ross, may collect, use, process, store, and share information when users access the App.

## 1. Scope
This Policy applies to Fantasy Football Matrix™ and its related web/app functionality. Third-party services used with the App have their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information the App May Process
Depending on enabled features, the App may process:

### Account and league information
- fantasy-platform username or account identifier;
- league ID, draft ID, roster ID, team identity, league settings, lineup settings, matchup information, and transaction/ownership data;
- rostered players, available players, league opponents, and related fantasy-football data retrieved from connected providers.

### App usage information
- feature interactions, settings, saved preferences, diagnostic events, and other usage information needed to operate, improve, secure, or troubleshoot the App.

### Device and technical information
- browser type, device type, operating system, IP-derived technical data, crash/error data, and similar information where such collection is enabled by hosting, analytics, security, or infrastructure providers.

### Payment information
If paid features are offered, payment details may be processed by a third-party payment processor. Fantasy Football Matrix™ is not intended to directly store full payment-card numbers.

### Communications
If you contact support, the App owner may retain your email address, correspondence, and information you voluntarily provide.

## 3. How Information May Be Used
Information may be used to:
- connect to fantasy-platform accounts or leagues at the user's request;
- synchronize rosters, matchups, player ownership, player status, and league settings;
- generate lineup, waiver, trade, roster, and opponent-analysis recommendations;
- remember settings and improve user experience;
- provide subscriptions, support, security, fraud prevention, and troubleshooting;
- improve features, reliability, and recommendation quality;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms;
- support future advertising, sponsorship, affiliate, or promotional features where permitted by law and disclosed appropriately.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The App may use or interact with third-party services such as fantasy platforms, data providers, hosting providers, analytics providers, payment processors, and other infrastructure vendors.

When the App retrieves data from a connected third party, that third party's terms and privacy policy also apply. Fantasy Football Matrix™ does not control third-party privacy practices.

## 5. Fantasy and Player Data
Fantasy rosters, league settings, matchup information, player names, injury designations, and player statistics may originate from third-party providers or public/licensed data sources. Such information is used to provide fantasy-team management and analysis features.

## 6. Advertising and Future Monetization
The App may introduce advertising, affiliate links, sponsorships, or other promotional features in the future. If future advertising requires additional tracking, profiling, cookies, identifiers, or sharing of personal information, this Policy will be updated and consent/opt-out mechanisms will be provided when required by applicable law.

## 7. Cookies and Local Storage
The App may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to preserve preferences, provider selections, connection state, settings, or other functionality. Some third-party providers may also use their own technologies under their policies.

## 8. Data Sharing
Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with:
- service providers operating hosting, infrastructure, payments, analytics, support, or security functions;
- third-party fantasy/data providers when needed to fulfill a user-requested integration;
- legal authorities or other parties when required by law, court order, valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety, or security;
- a successor entity in connection with a lawful merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of the App/business.

Fantasy Football Matrix™ does not claim ownership of a user's fantasy-platform account or third-party league data.

## 9. Data Retention
Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, legitimate business needs, security, dispute resolution, backups, or legal requirements. Some information may remain with third-party providers according to their own retention practices.

## 10. Security
Reasonable administrative and technical measures may be used to protect information. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

## 11. User Choices
Depending on the feature and applicable law, users may be able to:
- disconnect or stop using a third-party fantasy integration;
- clear locally stored App preferences through browser/device controls;
- request information about or correction/deletion of personal information directly controlled by the App owner;
- exercise applicable privacy rights under state or federal law.

Requests may be sent to Rossbrett05@gmail.com. Identity verification may be required where appropriate.

## 12. Children's Privacy
Fantasy Football Matrix™ is intended only for users age 18 or older and is not directed to children. The App does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

## 14. Third-Party Links
The App may link to third-party websites or services. This Policy does not govern those third parties.
